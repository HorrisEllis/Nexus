## 2. The meaning, by theme

### Leverage

*Reading:* The most power for the fewest tokens. A few primitives and invariants that overlap and combine into higher-leverage code; options over hard-coding; novel only when it is stable; and every piece enterprise grade, consistent and fully built, never half-built.

*Reading:* Systems, each sovereign: it owns its files, data and nodes, and one going down breaks no other. Components are dumb; the component registry is the spine that knows their wires. ## 7. How this was made

- **Found:** every `James…: "…"` in the two repositories (Markdown, specs, code, changelogs), including quotes chained after the first with `·` or `/`, quotes that wrap across lines, and the `> "…"` block quotes in Versionium's changelogs. Archives (`_archive/`), `data/` and `node_modules/` are not read.
- **Merged:** a quote that is a shortened copy of a longer one (the record often truncates with `…`) is merged into the longer one, and keeps every place it appears.
- **Dated:** by the date written beside the quote, else the nearest date before it in the same file. An undated quote has neither.
- **Indexed:** the words were counted per quote; the themed index groups his spellings of one word under one head (e.g. *dependancy*, *dependency*, *depenedancies*).
- **Limits, said plainly:** the extraction is by pattern, so a few entries are things he pasted (a log line, an error) or text he quoted back to choose an option; a quote never written down in either repository is not here. The themes and summaries are the coder's reading (to correct).
