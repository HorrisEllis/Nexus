## What it is

Emergence is James's language. `emerge/emerge.spec` is its grammar (6 roots, 7 axioms, 45 domains, the grammar held to its own rules:
GRAMMAR_APPLIES_TO_ITSELF). `emerge/emerge-kernel.js` parses a `.eg` program through a signal/noise gate and boots its compartments;
`emerge/emerge-codegen-v2.js` compiles one to a CommonJS module that requires only the bus primitive, with its bus and store handed in, so
a module written in Emergence is sovereign by construction. `emerge/core/` is his Rheon primitives as code (Field, Constraint, Transition,
Observation, Lens, Gap, History, Level, Budget, Seed). `emerge/schemas/` are the reusable node types.

warp is its runtime: `Event → Gate → Stream → StreamLog`, `Axiom`, and `unifiedDispatch` (exact cache, pre-generation filter, population,
cascade, axiom gate, score and retain, escalate). warp's own README: "Nothing in `core/`, `dispatch/`, or `plugins/` imports anything
outside this folder."

## How it is reached

- The language surface (`lib/system-boundary.js` LANGUAGE: `warp/core/`, `warp/dispatch/`, `warp/plugins/`, `warp/index.js`, `emerge/core/`)
  is used by nine systems; a use of it is kind `language`, not a crossing (EW_D2, decided by his axioms §5.9, §0.0, §1.2).
- Every other file of emerge/ or warp/ is the system's own; reaching into it is a crossing like any other.
- Moved out: the spec compiler is `idearium/spec-compiler/` (EW3, 0.60.239), reached from outside Idearium through `lib/spec-compiler-port.js`.
  Still to move: the Emerge IDE (`emerge/emerge-ide.js`, EW4).
- `emerge/siso/` is the language's bus (LANGUAGE): every module `emerge-codegen-v2.js` compiles requires it.

Open, found by the census (0.60.237): `emerge/core/history.js` and `emerge/core/level.js` require `intelligence/rfr2` (identity, time),
so the core is not yet the zero-dependency core its header claims (E10).

Tests: `warp/test/`, `tests/modules/test-emerge-core.test.js`, `tests/modules/test-warp2.test.js`.
