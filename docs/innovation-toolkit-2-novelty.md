When it comes to outside the box, idea generation, or creative ideas, take everything you know about being normal, and invert it. This is for ideas only, not a universal heuristic. 

Use contrast thinking to flip normalcy into novelty. Being weird is innovations greatest ally. Novelty lies in the contrast between two objects, people, edges and ideas. Creativity in the empty space beyond edges. 

Innovation in the combination of known, and making the unknown. Leverage lies in the amount of impact and vectors of your intention.  

Sometimes; you need to drop your defenses, the idea of perfection, or that ideas need to have a purpose. Ideas form more naturally, when they can exist with good or bad. Let structure stay in the invariants, and the fluidity in the uncertain. 

# Section 1 - Using Compartments.

#### Part 1 - Compartmentalizing constraints:

Ideas with the highest leverage and cleanest intent, are chosen for the compartment. The more reasons, meaning or intention you can find for the idea; the higher leverage. 

Compartments should mutate/integrate if the idea is no longer useful, or something else has replaced it. Or when moving on to another idea or it’s been externalized into something tangible. 

Take a compartment and call it whatever feels right for your inner child. This is a judgement free zone. Ideas from here are free from labels. This is your creative space. 

Then take another box and declare constraints and set axioms. Then copy any ideas you see as useful for your projects or life, write on a label and slap it on another compartment. Then believe it’s possible for that box to be full, but accept that it doesn’t have to be. 

#### Part 2 - C**ounterfactual construction.**

Try to imagine what that looks like, the conditions it would take for the outcome to exist.

This is called end-state first reasoning, and reverse causal chaining. This will help you find the gap between your mind and reality. Innovate from both sides. 

Find the conditions while thinking about what your next move is and that’s where you find solid ground and what survives reality. Anything that doesn’t give you momentum towards that end-goal, or would make a meaningful impact, should be tossed. If tension. 

Only use real tangibility as a core component for your ideas. Thats innovation. Mixing the known, and unknown. 

This works by creating an open loop, letting the minds curiousity, wonder or need for structure/certainty to fill in gaps.
