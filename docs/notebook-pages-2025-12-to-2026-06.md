## 3. Horizon Planning  *(02/23/26)*

Take the end-state and work backwards. Ask yourself, what conditions need to exist for this outcome to exist?

What does my home life look like in 5 years? What does work look like? Am I grinding, or building something? Who, or what kind of people do I want in that vision? What do I want to have accomplished? Skills, achievements, paths pursued. Don’t think about anyone else. Only you for now. Determine what the optimal outcome vs the minimal outcome is. Determine who you are when you look in the mirror. Who you are, What you stand for, What you won’t stand for. What do you want people to think about when you cross their mind? How do you want the people in your life to think of you? How do you want to feel, them to feel about you?

Synthesize those variables as a guide for inner-work. Embody who you want to be. Dont act, don’t perform, believe that’s who you’ll become. If you can’t at least believe it’s possible. It is possible. People have turned their life around from nothing. me included.
